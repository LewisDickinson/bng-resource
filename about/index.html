About

Hi I'm Lewis and I'm a nature conservation professional of 10 years specialising in land management, landscapes and wetland ecology. I am also building LAMOTT.

LAMOTT is a tool the makes tracking the implementation and compliance of nature finance schemes centralised and easy. Track a range of obligations, milestones, measures, risk, activity, and monitoring to ensure there is a robust record for delivery against the commitments made for any nature finance schemes.

I have built Lamott in my own time and it is a project of my freelance consultancy Spring Biodiversity. See more and follow the build at lamott.app.
