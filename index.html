Can this habitat bank actually afford to pay someone?

Biodiversity Net Gain units come with a 30-year delivery obligation. To deliver, you need to fund staff, overheads and habitat management costs that run every year until the obligation ends. These two models allow you to test the numbers, so you know before you commit whether the economics support hiring extra resource to deliver it.
